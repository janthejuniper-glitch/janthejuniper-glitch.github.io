Bio

Listo

Jan The Juniper es artista, productora y vocalista de Barcelona. Escribe, produce y canta su propia música, y la lleva al escenario en directos y sesiones como DJ.

Tras el EP 999 (2021), prepara COLLISION / SURRENDER, un EP de dos caras: COLLISION es la rabia, con una estética cyborg; SURRENDER es la vulnerabilidad, con una ciencia ficción de fantasía setentera.

Jan The Juniper is an artist, producer and vocalist from Barcelona, Spain. Jan writes, produces and sings their own music, and takes it to the stage in live shows and DJ sets.

After the 999 EP (2021), Jan is preparing COLLISION / SURRENDER, a two-sided EP: COLLISION is rage, with a cyborg aesthetic; SURRENDER is vulnerability, with 70s fantasy sci-fi.
